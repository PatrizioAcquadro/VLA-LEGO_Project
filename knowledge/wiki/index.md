# Alex003 Bimanual Assembly VLA

Build an ordered five-block tower with genuine bimanual cooperation in Isaac Sim/Lab, then on Purdue Alex with VLA inference entirely on Jetson AGX Orin. The admitted simulation corpus and executed learning campaign are delivered; learned assembly and qualification remain unmet. Start with [status](status.md).
